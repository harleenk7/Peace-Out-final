Privacy Policy

At Peace Out, we are committed to ensuring your privacy and protecting your personal data. We believe in providing a safe, mindful space for all our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website, tools, and community.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. This includes:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and contact information when you make a purchase or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Payment Data: Secure billing information handled entirely by our third-party payment processors. We do not store your credit card details.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our website to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us to:

  • Process your orders and deliver our mindfulness workbooks securely.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates and support.
  • Improve the features and functionality of our platform.
  • Ensure compliance with legal obligations.

3. Data Protection

We implement stringent security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. We only retain your data for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and fulfilling orders, strictly under confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. If you have questions about your privacy or wish to exercise your rights, please reach out to us at peaceoutt2025@gmail.com.

about the brand

A softer place to land.

Peace Out is a wellness brand and creative community for people who want to slow down, without falling behind. We build thoughtful tools and honest resources to help you breathe a little easier.

what we stand for

Three anchors behind everything we build.

01

Self-love and gratitude

Simple, repeatable practices that make self-love and gratitude part of an ordinary day, through reflective prompts, workbooks and gentle daily rituals.

02

Manifestation and vision

Vision boards and goal-setting grounded in clarity and action, so your intentions become plans you actually follow through on.

03

Everyday mindfulness

Accessible mindfulness for real life. Calm, present and easy to begin, with no perfect conditions or extra hours required.

what we do

The work behind the calm.

01

Digital Products

Workbooks, guides and digital downloads built on real exercises for mindful, intentional living.

02

Working With Coaches and Wellness Brands

We collaborate with mindfulness coaches, wellness brands and mental-health coaches, supporting them with content, resources and hands-on coaching.

03

Social Media and Community Building

Social content and community strategy that turn a following into a loyal, engaged community.

04

Workshops and Challenges

Guided workshops and community challenges, like 14 Days to Yourself, that bring people together around steady growth.

our story

Two Friends. One Shared Vision.

Drishti Malhotra

Drishti leads Peace Out's creative vision and brand voice, shaping how the whole community reads, sounds and feels.

A writer at heart, she has a gift for turning big, abstract ideas into words people hold onto: honest stories, gentle prompts and quiet tools for reflection.

She believes wellness should feel like a warm place you come back to, rather than one more task on the list and she writes everything with that in mind. Somewhere in every piece of Peace Out, you will find her voice.

Harleen Kaur

Harleen leads product and strategy, turning big ideas into tools people can actually use and small daily practices that hold up in a busy life.

She pairs a sharp eye for detail with real warmth, shaping the resources, collaborations and partnerships that carry Peace Out out into the world.

Grounded in her own practice of mindfulness and intentional growth, she builds things that make peace feel practical and genuinely within reach.
